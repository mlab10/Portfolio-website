How I Work

I've had a bit of experience in UI/UX design and development. I participated in a few hackathons and created web applications in groups projects for school. Now, I am taking a web development course to further develop my skills and learn new ones.

When working with me, transparency, honesty and team work go a long way. I value direct feedback, early collaboration, and discussions focused on solving the problem rather than assigning blame. I prefer organized communication and clear expectations, but I’m flexible in how we work together. Written communication is often helpful because it gives space to think through ideas carefully, especially for complex problems. At the same time, I enjoy collaborative discussions when brainstorming or troubleshooting challenges together.
